1. What this service is

Nibble Tech LLC ("Nibbletec") provides a remote technical support session in view-only mode. During the session you share your screen from your browser, and the technician tells you what to do by pointing to areas of your screen. Optionally, you can use a shared browser in which the technician can act if you authorize it (section 3).

2. What the technician can and can’t do

  • They can see only what you choose to share: your whole screen, one window, or one tab.
  • They can point, highlight, and write directions that you will see on top of the image of your screen.
  • They can’t move your mouse, type on your keyboard, open or copy your files, or install software on your computer.

3. Shared browser (optional)

If you open it, the service gives you a web browser that runs on Nibbletec’s servers and that you and the technician see at the same time. This browser has no access to your computer, your files, or your other windows.

  • The technician can click, type, or navigate in it only when you give them control. You can take control back at any time with the "Take back control" button or by closing the shared browser.
  • Actions the technician takes while in control are taken on your behalf and with your authorization. [Adjust this clause with your attorney.]
  • The technician sees everything shown and typed in this browser, including passwords. We recommend typing your passwords yourself and not entering data that isn’t needed.
  • Files can’t be downloaded or uploaded.
  • When you close the shared browser or the session ends, its cookies, history, and any sign-ins are deleted.

4. You stay in control

You decide what to share and can stop sharing at any time with the "Stop sharing" button or your browser’s control. You perform every action on your computer yourself.

5. Confidential information

Before sharing, we recommend closing windows that show passwords, banking details, medical information, or other people’s personal data that isn’t needed to solve the problem. If such information appears on screen, the technician will not record it or use it for any purpose other than providing this service.

6. Recording and screenshots

The session is not recorded. To document the case, the technician may take occasional screenshots of the shared screen; you will see a notice each time. Screenshots are kept for [90 days] and then deleted.

7. Data we record

To keep proof of your consent and for security, we record your name, the date and time you accepted, your IP address, your browser information, and the version of these terms. If you use the shared browser, we also record the websites (domains) visited and when control was given or taken back. We keep this record for [3 years].

8. Transmission security

The image of your screen travels encrypted between your browser and the technician’s. When your network requires it, it passes through a Nibbletec relay server that cannot decrypt it. The image of the shared browser travels encrypted between Nibbletec’s servers and your browser.

9. Authorization

By accepting, you confirm that you are an authorized user of this computer or that you have permission from its owner or your employer to share its screen.

10. Liability

The technician’s directions are guidance, and you decide whether to follow them. We recommend keeping backups of your information. [Adjust this clause with your attorney.]
